Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the photo or date after going live?
No — the photo and party date are locked once your invite is live. If you need to change either, delete the party from the link in your confirmation email and create a new one. It only takes a minute.
Should I print the QR code or share the link?
The link is the most reliable way to share — it works perfectly every time via WhatsApp, text, or email. If you'd like to print the QR code on cards, keep it at least the size of a large postage stamp and place it on a plain, uncluttered background so it scans reliably.
How do I reach my guests in an emergency?
From your Party Page you can message all confirmed guests instantly. If you need their contact details urgently, use the export button on your Party Page to get the full guest list with email addresses sent to your inbox.
Are guests reminded before the party?
Yes. Everyone who RSVPs yes receives an automatic reminder email 3 days before the party. You can also add a personal note to that reminder from your Party Page — something like parking details or what to bring.
Can I lock the guest list so no last-minute changes come in?
Yes. When you create your invite you can set an RSVP cutoff date — by default 8 days before the party, which is when most venues ask for final numbers. After that date, the invite automatically closes and guests see a friendly message letting them know RSVPs are no longer accepted. You'll also receive your final guest list with a CSV file on that day. The cutoff can only be set at creation, not afterwards.
What if a guest changes their mind?
They can reopen the same invite link, enter the same name they used before, and resubmit. Their RSVP updates automatically and you'll get a notification that their response changed. Once the RSVP cutoff date has passed, changes are no longer accepted.
Can I message all my guests at once?
Yes. From your Party Page you can email everyone who's RSVP'd yes — handy if details change, like a venue update or a parking reminder. It goes straight to each guest's inbox.
Can I edit a guest's details if they contact me directly?
Yes. Your Party Page lets you update any guest's name, attendance, or guest count manually — useful when someone messages you instead of using the link.
What if I lose my Party Page link?
Your Party Page link was emailed to you when you created the invite, so check that email to get back in. Keep it safe — it's your private link for managing RSVPs.
It's free — is there a catch?
No catch. If you buy something through our party shop links we may earn a small commission, at no extra cost to you — that's what keeps the site free. We may add the occasional partner promotion over time, but we never sell your data, and guests never see ads on the RSVP page.
