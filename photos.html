Questions

Good to know.

How do I book?

Click “I want the experience”. It opens an email to me, ready to go: add your date and where, and I'll reply with availability, usually the same day.

Are you insured?

Yes, £5m public liability with Chiswell. I can send the certificate to your venue.

How much space is needed?

About 2m × 2m near a plug socket.

Is it good for young children?

Yes, it was made with kids' parties in mind.

How do guests get their photos?

Straight away, by AirDrop or by scanning a QR code, and every photo also goes into your online gallery.

Can it be personalised?

Yes. The ring light can glow red, green, blue or rainbow, and the photo layout can be designed around your event. Both packages include a custom start screen with your names, date and colours, or your own design.

Is the selfie pod a photo booth?

Yes. It's a modern, open-style photo booth: guests take their own photos, strips and GIFs with studio lighting, then get them straight away. I cover Warwickshire, Gloucestershire and everywhere within about 50 miles of Stratford-upon-Avon.

Do you work with venues?

Yes, venues are welcome to get in touch.
