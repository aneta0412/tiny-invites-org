Why I started this

I was always too busy enjoying the party to take photos

I love kids' birthday parties. But every time, I'd get home and realise I had barely any photos, and almost none of the guests.

Hiring a photographer felt complicated: packages, add-ons, and weeks of waiting for edited pictures. I wanted something simpler that still caught all the fun.

So I started a selfie booth. Your guests snap themselves, the booth catches every giggle, every silly face and every guest, and you get to actually enjoy your own party.

— An, Stratford-upon-Avon

Questions

Good to know

Are you insured?

Yes. I hold up to £5 million public liability insurance with Chiswell, and I'm happy to send a copy of the certificate to you or your venue before the event.

How much space does the booth need?

Not much at all, roughly a 2m × 2m corner near a plug socket. It works well in party halls, soft play centres, marquees, function rooms and at home.

Is it suitable for young children?

Yes! It was made with kids' parties in mind. Little ones love seeing themselves on the screen, and it's simple enough for them to use with a grown-up nearby.

How do guests get their photos?

Guests can send their photos to their own phone straight from the booth, and you'll receive the full set of photos from your event afterwards.

Can guests get printed photos?

Yes, add the instant print option and a Canon SELPHY Square printer prints photos at the party. Each one is a 68 × 68 mm sticker-backed square, ideal for party bags, guest books or the fridge. Choose the printer with 20 prints for £50, or with up to 50 prints for £85.

How long is the hire?

Standard hire is 3 hours of unlimited photos, plus set-up and pack-down time that doesn't count towards your hours. Weekday parties (Monday to Thursday) are discounted, from £180.

Do you travel to my venue?

I cover Stratford-upon-Avon and up to 50 miles around. Travel within 15 miles is free, with a small flat fee beyond that. Further afield? Just ask.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can, especially for Saturdays and wedding season. Send an enquiry and I'll let you know straight away if your date is free.

Do you work with venues?

Yes. If you run a party venue or wedding venue and would like to offer the booth to your customers, I'd love to hear from you.
