Internet test
Run it at the venue before guests arrive, with the iPad where the booth will stand. It takes about ten seconds.

    The rule

    Online only when a guest’s QR code would take under 15 seconds. Slower than that counts as offline, exactly like no internet: no QR codes and no uploads, so nobody ever waits. AirDrop, email and the guest book work as normal, and every photo waits safely on the iPad until the internet is fast again.

    Practise

    Earlier tests

      Online: QR codes on, and photos go to the online gallery as guests finish.
      Offline (no internet, or too slow): AirDrop, email and the guest book work as normal; photos are kept on the iPad and go online by themselves once it is fast enough. The booth checks again every couple of minutes.
      The badge in the top right corner of the start screen always shows which one it is.
      Quick booth settings

      Booth status

        The badge in the top right corner of the start screen shows Online or Offline (online only when a guest’s QR code would take under 15 seconds). For the internet test at any time, tap the top left corner of the start screen three times. For a long event, set Auto-Lock to Never: Settings, Display and Brightness, Auto-Lock.
