Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Tiny Invites collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what your rights are. It covers tinyinvites.org (including our invite and RSVP pages, Party Finder and suppliers directory) and photos.tinyinvites.org (our Selfie Pod hire).

The short version

  • We only collect what we need to run your party page, reply to you, or bring the Selfie Pod to your event.
  • We never sell your details, and we never use them for marketing.
  • Hosts see their guests' replies. Guests see the party details the host chooses to add.
  • Analytics cookies are only used if you click Accept on our cookie banner.
  • Party pages and all their replies are deleted automatically 60 days after the party, and hosts can delete them sooner.
  • Questions or requests? Email hello@tinyinvites.org.

2. Party pages: if you're the host

What we collect
Your email address; your child's first name and the age they're turning; the party date, start time, length and venue; your RSVP deadline; and, if you add them, a special note for guests, a party photo link and your phone number. We also keep what you do in your dashboard, such as reminder notes and updates you send to guests.
Why
To create your party page, confirm your email, send you your welcome email with your RSVP link and QR code, tell you when guests reply, send the reminders and updates you ask for, and email you the final guest list.
Lawful basis
It's needed to provide the service you've asked us for (contract).
  • Your email address is never shown to guests. Guests who want to contact you send a message through your party page, and we pass it on.
  • Your phone number is only shown on your party page if you tick the box to share it.
  • If you add a party photo using a Google Photos link, the photo stays on Google Photos; we only store the link.
  • When a new party page is created, we receive a short notification email (your child's first name, age, the party date and venue) so we can spot problems and help if something goes wrong. Our lawful basis for this is our legitimate interest in keeping the service working.

3. Party pages: if you're a guest

What we collect
Your name and email address; whether you're coming and how many adults, children and babies; whether you'd like a reminder; and, only if you choose to add them, allergies or dietary requirements and any note or message for the host.
Why
To pass your reply to the host, email you a confirmation, send you a reminder before the party and any updates from the host, and pass on your messages.
Lawful basis
Our legitimate interest in helping the host organise the party you're replying to. For allergy and dietary information, your explicit consent (see below).
Allergies and dietary needs. This can be health information, which has extra protection under data protection law. We only collect it if you choose to give it. We share it only with the host of that party, and it's deleted along with the party page. You can ask the host or us to remove it at any time.

Your email address is only used for this party: for your confirmation, a reminder before the party and updates from the host. It's never used for marketing. Every reminder email has an unsubscribe link.

4. Contact and enquiry forms

What we collect
Your name, email address and whatever you tell us. For Selfie Pod enquiries, also your event date, type of event, package, venue or town, approximate number of guests and, if you give it, your phone number.
Why
To reply to you, give you a quote and, if you book, to arrange and run your booking (including invoices and payments).
Lawful basis
Taking steps at your request before a contract and then carrying out the contract. For general questions, our legitimate interest in answering you.

Our forms are handled by Formspree, which delivers your message to our inbox.

5. The Selfie Pod at your event

At events where we provide the Selfie Pod, guests take their own photos and short animations (GIFs), and can leave messages in a digital guest book. These usually show the faces of the people who took them, including children.

  • Photos are saved on the pod's iPad and uploaded to Cloudinary, the service that hosts your event's online gallery.
  • Guests can save a photo by AirDrop or by scanning its QR code. The host receives a link to the online gallery of every photo.
  • Anyone with the gallery link, or with a photo's QR code, can view and download those photos. Please only share the gallery link with people you're happy to see them.
  • We never use event photos on our website or social media without the host's permission. We will not use a photo of a child without the permission of their parent or carer.

Lawful basis: providing the service the host booked (contract with the host), and our legitimate interest in running the pod for the host's guests. If you're a host, please let your guests know the Selfie Pod is there and that photos go into a shared online gallery. If you appear in a photo and want it removed, contact the host or email us.

6. Party Finder and suppliers directory

  • Searching: the postcode or town you type is used in your browser to sort venues by distance. It isn't sent to us.
  • The map: map images come from OpenStreetMap, so your browser connects to their servers, which see your IP address.
  • During your visit: Party Finder remembers which venues it showed you using your browser's session storage. This is cleared when you close the tab.
  • Business listings: we list venues and party suppliers using information they publish themselves, such as their websites and public business listings. Some listings include the names of the people who run the business. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in helping parents find local services. If you run a listed business and want your listing corrected or removed, email us and we'll sort it out.

7. Analytics, cookies and links to other sites

Google Analytics, only if you accept

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand how our pages are used, so we can improve them. When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Google Analytics only loads if you click Accept. If you click Reject, or don't choose, it doesn't load and no analytics cookies are set.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_…) and collects information such as the pages you visit, the links you click, the type of device and browser you use, and your approximate location (city or country). Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses. On the Selfie Pod page it also records when an enquiry is sent and the type of event chosen, but not your name or contact details.

Our lawful basis is your consent. You can change your mind at any time using Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw your consent, we delete the analytics cookies.

Storage that makes the site work

These are essential for the site to work, so we don't need to ask for permission:

  • Your cookie choice: a small cookie called ti_cookie_consent remembers whether you accepted or rejected analytics, for 12 months, on both tinyinvites.org and photos.tinyinvites.org. A copy is also kept in your browser's local storage.
  • Session storage: some pages remember things just for your visit, for example which venues you've already seen in Party Finder or that you've just sent an RSVP. This is cleared when you close the tab.

Shop and affiliate links

Some links in our shop and gift ideas are affiliate links to Amazon and to retailers through the Awin network. If you click one, the retailer may set cookies to record that you came from Tiny Invites, so we can earn a small commission. We don't receive your name or contact details. Once you're on their site, the retailer's own privacy policy applies.

Fonts, code and other sites

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from cdnjs (run by Cloudflare), so your browser connects to their servers, which see your IP address. Links to other websites, such as venues, suppliers and print partners, are covered by those websites' own privacy policies.

8. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information and we never share it for marketing. We share it only:

  • Between hosts and guests, as described above: hosts see their guests' replies, and guests see the party details the host adds.
  • With the service providers that run parts of Tiny Invites for us. They may only use your information to provide their service to us:
Vercel
Website hosting and the code behind our forms and emails
Supabase
Our database for party pages, replies and venue listings
Resend
Sending emails, such as confirmations, notifications and reminders
Upstash
Protecting our forms from spam and abuse. This briefly processes IP addresses to limit how often a form can be sent.
Formspree
Our contact and Selfie Pod enquiry forms
Cloudinary
Storing and showing Selfie Pod photos and the online gallery
Google
Google Analytics and Google Fonts, and Google Photos if a host adds a photo link
Cloudflare and OpenStreetMap
Code libraries and the Party Finder map
  • When the law requires it, or where it's needed to protect someone's safety.

9. Information stored outside the UK

Some of the providers above are based in, or use servers in, the United States or other countries outside the UK. When your information is transferred outside the UK, we rely on the protections UK law allows. These include UK "adequacy" regulations (such as the UK–US data bridge for certified US companies) and the providers' standard data protection contract terms.

10. How long we keep it

Party pages and replies
Deleted automatically 60 days after the party date, together with every guest reply, allergy note and message. Party pages that were never confirmed are deleted after 30 days. Hosts can delete their party page and all replies at any time using the link in their emails.
Party notification emails
Our copies are deleted once we no longer need them, normally within 60 days of the party.
Enquiries
Kept for up to 12 months if you don't go on to book.
Bookings
Booking records and invoices are kept for 6 years, as UK tax rules require.
Selfie Pod photos
The online gallery stays available for 3 months after your event so you and your guests can download the photos. After that, we delete the photos from the gallery and from the pod.
Analytics
If you accept analytics cookies, the data is kept in Google Analytics for up to 14 months.
Your cookie choice
Remembered for 12 months, then we ask again.

11. Keeping it safe

  • All our pages use a secure, encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Your party dashboard is opened with a private link that we email to you. Anyone with that link can manage your party, so please keep it to yourself. If you think someone else has it, email us.
  • Guest replies are only visible to the host. Guests only see the party details the host chooses to add.
  • We limit who and what can access your information to the service providers listed above, and only for what they do for us.

12. Children

Tiny Invites is for parents and carers. A party page includes a child's first name and the age they're turning, which the parent or carer adds. Replies should be sent by a parent or carer. We don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you think a child has given us their details, please email us and we'll delete them. Photos of children taken with the Selfie Pod are covered in section 5.

13. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that's wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to us using it;
  • ask for your information in a format you can take elsewhere;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, for example to us holding allergy information you gave us.

To use any of these rights, email hello@tinyinvites.org. We may need to check who you are before we act. There's normally no charge, and we'll reply within one month. If you're a guest, you can also ask the host to change or remove your reply.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.
