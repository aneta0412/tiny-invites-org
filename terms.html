6. Selfie Pod hire

This section applies when you book the Tiny Invites Selfie Pod through photos.tinyinvites.org or by contacting us.

Booking

  • We'll confirm your package, hire time, price and venue by email. That email is your booking confirmation.
  • Your date is reserved once we receive your deposit. The balance is due by the date given in your booking confirmation.

If you need to cancel or change your date

  • Please tell us as soon as you can.
  • Your deposit holds your date, and we turn other bookings away for it, so it can't be refunded if you cancel. Anything you've paid on top of the deposit will be refunded.
  • If you'd like to move your booking to another date, we'll do our best to help, subject to availability.
  • Because each booking is for a leisure service on a specific date, the 14-day cancellation period that usually applies to bookings made online or by phone doesn't apply (regulation 28 of the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013). The cancellation terms in this section apply instead.

If we have to cancel

This is very unlikely. But if illness, an emergency or an equipment failure means we can't come, we'll tell you as soon as possible, try to arrange an alternative, and refund everything you've paid, including your deposit.

Space, power and set-up

  • The pod needs a space of about 2m × 2m, indoors or under cover, near a standard plug socket.
  • Please make sure we have the venue's permission to set up, and access at the agreed time.
  • We set up before your guests arrive and pack away afterwards. Your hire time runs for the agreed period. If we can't get into the venue on time for reasons outside our control, we can't always extend it.

Looking after the pod

Please make sure guests, especially young children, use the pod sensibly and are supervised. If the pod or its equipment is damaged through misuse at your event, we may charge the reasonable cost of repair.

Photos and the online gallery

  • Guests take their own photos at the pod. Please let your guests know the pod is there and that photos go into a shared online gallery.
  • The online gallery stays available for 3 months after your event. Please download any photos you want to keep before then.
  • We never use photos from your event in our marketing without your permission. More details are in our privacy policy.

Insurance

We hold £5m public liability insurance and can send the certificate to your venue on request.
