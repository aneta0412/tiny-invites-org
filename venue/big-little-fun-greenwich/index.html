About this venue

Greenwich's dedicated under-5s soft play café, designed from the ground up for babies and toddlers. Sensory room (SEN and autism-friendly), role play area, arts and puzzles area, selfie booth, sing-along and baby gym classes, story time and more. Birthday party packages are exclusively for the 0–5 age group — no older children — making it ideal for toddler groups. Packages include a party host, inflatable castle, face painting, food, party bags, printed invitations, and a visit from Big Bear Cool Bear the mascot. Staff handle all setup and cleanup.
