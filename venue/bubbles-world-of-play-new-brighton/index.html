About this venue

One of the UK's biggest indoor play centres with over 30 themed educational soft play zones including Dino Land, Wild West cannon zone, Northern Lights, and a New York-themed baby and toddler area. Birthday parties include 90 minutes play plus hot and cold buffet in a private themed party booth (underwater, safari, space, etc.), party host, invitations, and reserved adult seating. Also offers budget-friendly Play Dates without the party extras. Free car park with 90+ spaces.
