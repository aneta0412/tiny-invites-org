About this venue

Birthday parties at Dudley Zoo and Castle — a 40-acre zoo built around an 11th-century castle in the heart of the West Midlands. Two party options: the Party Room Hire package gives exclusive use of the Meerkat and Lorikeet Rooms for 2 hours with zoo admission included for up to 40 guests, or the Animal Encounter Party gets the children up close to snakes, lizards, chinchillas and small animals, with craft activities, a personalised birthday cake, party lunch bags, gift bags crammed with zoo merchandise, a free family return ticket for the birthday child, and a visit from Dudley the zoo mascot. Reviewed as outstanding value — face painting, land train and adventure playground also on site.
