About this venue

Warrington's long-established family-run soft play centre, founded in 2008 and managed by a former Early Years teacher — so you know the focus on child safety and development is genuine. Located on the 3rd floor of Evans House in Warrington town centre, 5 minutes walk from Warrington Central station. Features a two-tier one-way soft play frame (not too scary for little ones), low-height climbing wall, two slides, pirate ship, crawl tubes, rockers, the largest designated under-4s area in the area, a large sports pitch, and a bouncy slide. Birthday parties from £12 per child with themed options including Pirate/Princess, Messy Play, Giddy Giggles and VIP packages. Exclusive evening hire also available. Toddler activities run Mon–Fri 10–12 (messy play, arts and crafts, sensory). Adults go free.
