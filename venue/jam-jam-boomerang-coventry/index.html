About this venue

Coventry's biggest indoor play and party venue, established since 2007 with a 5-star food hygiene rating every single year. Three-floor play frame for ages 4–12 with bridges, rollers, aerial glides, bish bags, a 4-lane wave slide and the DepthDrop fast slide. A separate two-floor under-4s area with a car track, Little Tikes cars and a baby pad for the very small. Football and basketball zone. Laser tag arena. Toddler mornings run term-time Mon–Fri 9:30–11:30am. Four birthday party packages including laser tag. Every party includes a Jam Jam celebration chocolate and ice cream cake, party invitations, a party helper, food and unlimited juice.
