About this venue

The biggest trampoline and adventure park in Warwickshire — located at Tachbrook Park, just off the A452 between Leamington Spa and Warwick. Formerly known as Go Jump In, now operating as AirHop. Features wall-to-wall trampolines, foam pit, giant inflatable, slide park (launch, whoop and racing slides), interactive sports pitch, Wipeout, climbing wall, and soft play area for under-6s with mini trampolines, baby zone, and two slides. Birthday parties: 4 packages across all ages. 20% off Mon–Fri term-time parties with code WEEKDAY20. Min 10 guests for all parties. Advance online booking gives lower price.
